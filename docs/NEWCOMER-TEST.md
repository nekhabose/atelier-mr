# Newcomer test (15 minutes)

One person who has never seen Atelier, ideally someone who does not use VR
much, shows more than another hour of polishing. Judges arrive cold too.

## Set-up

- **With a Quest:** open https://atelier-mr.vercel.app in the Quest Browser,
  clear the site's data first (so they get the first visit), hand them the
  headset with controllers put away. Cast to a phone or laptop so you can
  watch (Quest: Camera > Cast).
- **Without a Quest:** the same link on a laptop. Let them use the mouse and
  the start page; the short tour is fair game if they find it.

## What you say

Only this: *"This is a fitting room app. Get yourself an outfit for tomorrow."*
Then stay quiet. Do not point, explain or rescue for the first five minutes,
even when they are stuck. Being stuck is the finding.

## Three questions afterwards

1. In one sentence, what is Atelier for?
2. What was the most confusing moment?
3. Would you open it tomorrow morning? Why, or why not?

## Afterwards

Send me the notes (or just the three answers and where they got stuck). I fix
the first confusing moment before the deadline. If they say something good
about it, ask whether you may quote them on Devpost, using their first name
only.
